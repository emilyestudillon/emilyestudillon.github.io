About This Site

A fan-made tribute to Attack on Titan, created for fellow fans.

Why I Made This

This website was created to celebrate the story, characters, and world of Attack on Titan. The series has a unique way of combining action, mystery, and emotional storytelling, and I wanted to create a space where fans could explore the series in a clean and simple format. This site is meant to be a tribute to the show’s powerful themes and unforgettable moments.

What You Can Find

On this site, you can explore each season, learn about the most iconic characters, and dive deeper into the world beyond the walls. Each section is designed to be easy to navigate, with short summaries and key information that fans will recognize. Whether you are rewatching the series or discovering it for the first time, this site is built to help you explore the story in a meaningful way.

About the Style

The design of this site is inspired by the dark and intense tone of Attack on Titan. The dark background and bold typography reflect the series’ themes of survival and conflict. The goal is to create a look that feels dramatic and cinematic while still staying clean and easy to read.

Thank You

Thank you for visiting this fan site. I hope you enjoy exploring the world of Attack on Titan and learning more about the characters and story. If you have any ideas for improvements or additions, feel free to share them. This site is for fans, by fans — and it’s always open to new updates.
