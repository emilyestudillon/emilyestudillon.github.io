The Seasons

Each season of Attack on Titan shifts the story in unexpected and unforgettable ways.

Season 1

The first season introduces humanity’s fight against the Titans and follows Eren Yeager as he joins the Scout Regiment. This season sets up the mystery, fear, and action that define the series.

Season 2

Season two reveals shocking truths about the Titans themselves and focuses heavily on character backstories, especially Reiner, Bertholdt, and Ymir.

Season 3

This season explores political corruption within the walls and uncovers the true history of the world, completely changing how viewers see the story.

Final Season

The final season expands the world beyond the walls and dives into themes of war, freedom, and morality, blurring the line between heroes and villains.
