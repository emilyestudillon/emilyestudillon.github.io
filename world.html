The World of Attack on Titan

From the walls of Paradis to the outside world, the setting defines the story.

Paradis Island

Paradis Island is the home of the last remnants of humanity. The people live inside massive walls to protect themselves from the Titans.

The Walls

The walls—Maria, Rose, and Sina—protect humanity from Titans. Each wall is a massive barrier that defines the lives of the people inside.

The Outside World

The world beyond Paradis is full of unknown dangers and political conflicts, shaping the series’ biggest mysteries.
